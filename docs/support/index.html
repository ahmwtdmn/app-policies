Developer support

Let’s get it sorted.

For help with an app by Ahmet Duman, or to ask a question about your privacy, email:

ahmwtdmn@gmail.com

What to include

Tell us the app name, app version, device model and operating-system version, along with what happened and what you expected. Share screenshots only if you are comfortable doing so, and remove private information first.

Purchase support

For an access issue, include the app and product name and describe what the purchase or restore screen shows. Please keep passwords, payment-card details and verification codes out of your message. Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address for questions or requests about information held by the developer. The relevant app privacy policy explains local storage and any service-provider records that may be involved.

Türkçe destek

Uygulamalarla ilgili yardım ve gizlilik taleplerin için yukarıdaki adrese Türkçe yazabilirsin. Uygulamanın adını, sürümünü, cihaz modelini ve sorunun açıklamasını ekle. Şifre, doğrulama kodu veya kart bilgisi gönderme.
