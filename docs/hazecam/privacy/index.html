HazeCam is a camera and photo editor provided by Ahmet Duman. This policy applies specifically to HazeCam.

At a glance: Photo effects are processed on your device. HazeCam does not require an app account. An optional HazeCam+ Lifetime purchase is handled by Apple, with Adapty providing purchase validation, access management and purchase/product analytics.

Photos and camera

HazeCam uses camera access to capture photos and photo-library access to display, edit and save photos you choose to use. Photo effects are processed on your device. HazeCam does not upload your photos to a developer server or an AI service. Photos in your system photo library remain subject to your device and iCloud Photos settings.

Editing recipes, preferences, custom film settings and temporary recovery copies are stored locally on your device. You can remove saved photos in Photos, revoke camera or photo permissions in iOS Settings, and remove the app to remove its local app data. Removing the app does not delete photos saved in your system photo library.

Optional HazeCam+ purchase

HazeCam+ Lifetime is an optional one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. We use Adapty to load offers, validate purchases, restore purchases and determine premium access.

Adapty processes purchase history, device/app-install identifiers and product interactions such as app opens and purchase-screen views. These support purchase/access functionality and product/revenue analytics. We do not provide your name, email address, phone number or a developer-assigned customer user ID to Adapty. HazeCam does not require an app account.

Advertising identifier (IDFA) collection, IP-address collection and Adapty Attribution are disabled in HazeCam’s SDK configuration. Network and hosting providers may still process technical request data needed to deliver their services.

HazeCam does not use this data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Apple and Adapty process information under their respective Apple Privacy Policy and Adapty Privacy Policy. Data may be processed outside your country, including through these providers’ infrastructure.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive the address and content you send, and any diagnostic information you choose to include. This is used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private photos or payment details unless necessary for your support request.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Purchase and access records may be retained by Apple and Adapty as necessary to validate Lifetime access, restore purchases, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to request access to or deletion of information held by the developer. Some transaction records may need to be retained by the responsible provider for legal or purchase-validation reasons. You may also contact Apple or Adapty about information they hold under their policies.

Children and security

HazeCam is a general-audience photography tool and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request children’s contact details. We use appropriate safeguards and providers’ secured connections, although no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These policy pages contain no developer-added analytics or advertising scripts and no contact forms. This website hosting is separate from HazeCam’s on-device photo processing.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when HazeCam’s practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
